About This Curriculum

This 12-lesson, 5-day curriculum is a prerequisite-level primer for anyone entirely new to the maritime industry — no prior shipping background assumed. It builds the industry, operational, and regulatory literacy a learner needs before specializing further: vessel types and the trades they serve, how the global shipping industry actually commissions a voyage, port and terminal operations, navigation and cargo work aboard, ship construction in plain language, and the regulatory and commercial ecosystem (IMO, flag states, class societies, P&I clubs, SOLAS/MARPOL/STCW, the ISM and ISPS Codes) that governs all of it.

It pairs plain-language material with worked examples, mapping activities, and browser-based simulations, and it is designed as a companion and prerequisite to Maritime Operational Technology Security — this course builds the industry fluency that course assumes.

Scope & Intent

Every activity and simulation in this course runs on synthesized scenarios — none of it uses real vessel, port, or company data. The full policy is documented on the Safeguards & Responsible Use page, and it applies to every lesson linked from the course navigation.
